## Institutional Terminology (Source of Truth)

### Break of Structure [VIDEO 4, 6, 9, ART-09]
- **Definition**: A candle CLOSES beyond the previous swing high (bullish) or swing low (bearish) in the direction of the trend.

### Change of Character [VIDEO 4, 15, ART-09, ART-10]
- **Definition**: The first candle CLOSE beyond the most recent opposite swing against the prior trend (e.g. close through protected low in uptrend or close above lower high after sweep).

### Protected Low (Bullish) / Protected High (Bearish) [VIDEO 3, ART-02]
- **Definition**: The swing that started the move that broke structure. Selected from intermediate-term swings. While it holds, structure is intact.

### Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) / Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) [VIDEO 7, ART-04]
- **Definition**: Resting buy stops and breakout orders above old highs (BSL); resting sell stops and breakout orders below old lows (SSL). Pools are a map, not a promise.

### Liquidity Sweep [VIDEO 5, 6, ART-10]
- **Definition**: A wick penetration through a liquidity level followed by a candle close back inside the prior range.

### Fair Value Gap / Imbalance [VIDEO 5, 11, ART-08]
- **Definition**: Three-candle price imbalance where candle 1 wick and candle 3 wick do not overlap. Midpoint (50%) is equilibrium. Invalidated on candle close through gap.

### Order Block (OB) [VIDEO 1, ART-09]
- **Definition**: The last opposite-colour candle before a strong move that breaks structure AND leaves an FVG. Zone = high to low.

### Inversion Fair Value Gap [VIDEO 8]
- **Definition**: A broken FVG that flips role on return: old support becomes resistance (bearish inversion) or old resistance becomes support.

### Premium / Discount Equilibrium [VIDEO 2, ART-07]
- **Definition**: Dividing the swing range in half (50% equilibrium). Buy in discount, sell in premium. Range invalid on close beyond swing extreme.

### External vs Internal Range Liquidity [ART-03]
- **Definition**: ERL = stops outside range (EQH/EQL, PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL). IRL = FVGs and minor swings inside range. Price tends to loop from ERL to IRL, then IRL to ERL.

### Swing Hierarchy (STH/ITH/LTH) [ART-02]
- **Definition**: Rank 1 STH/STL (n=1 each side). Rank 2 ITH/ITL (lower STHs each side). Rank 3 LTH/LTL (lower ITHs each side). Confirmed-only; no repainting.

### Five-Check Master Model (Setup A) [ART-06]
- **Definition**: 1. Sweep -> 2. Structure (CHOCH) -> 3. Range (Discount) -> 4. Entry (FVG+OB) -> 5. Plan (SL below sweep, TP at equal highs). All 5 or no trade.
